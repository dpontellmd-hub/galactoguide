Delete your GalactoGuide account

You can request deletion without installing the app. Never include your password, sign-in code, or medical information in a request.

Delete in the app or on the website

Open Delete account, sign in, and verify your identity. Review the account and the consequences, then type DELETE and select “Permanently delete my account.” Until you confirm, you can cancel and keep your account.

Request help or deletion by email

If you cannot sign in, automatic deletion is unavailable, or your account uses Apple sign-in, email adam@dyadhealthcollective.com with the subject “GalactoGuide account deletion request” and the email used for your account. This opens your email app; it does not send a message automatically.

Support must verify ownership before any destructive action. Sending an email alone does not delete an account. Support will explain the verification steps and any records that need separate handling. Do not send credentials or identity documents unless a reviewed verification process specifically requires them.

What account deletion covers

The proposed automatic flow removes your sign-in account, synced saved substances, selected situations, preferences, and helpful votes. Your own discussion titles, text, and author identity are replaced with deleted-content placeholders; other people's responses remain. Reports by or about the deleted account and related report audit records are removed from the active database.

Public copies made by others, previous support emails, service logs, and backups are separate from active account data. Ask support about these records. The automatic flow is disabled until the retention policy and operator setup are approved. Apple authorization must also be revoked before an Apple-linked account can be deleted.
