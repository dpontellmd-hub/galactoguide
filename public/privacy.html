Privacy Policy

Updated September 19, 2026 · Dyad Health Collective LLC

You can browse the reference without an account. If you sign in, saved entries and selected situations sync with your account. Community posts are public. This policy describes the website’s current account, community, and feedback features.

1. Who we are

GalactoGuide is operated by Dyad Health Collective LLC in Nashville, Tennessee. For privacy questions or requests, contact us through dyadhealthcollective.com.

2. Accounts and sign-in

If you create an account with email and a password, Supabase processes your sign-in credentials and stores your account information, including your email address and account identifier. If you choose Google sign-in, Google and Supabase process that sign-in and the basic profile information Google shares, such as your name and email address. Browser storage keeps your session so you can remain signed in.

3. Saved entries and preferences

The website stores preferences on your device, including your parent/caregiver or provider view, disclaimer acknowledgment, selected situations, and appearance. When you sign in, your view, disclaimer acknowledgment, selected situations, and saved reference entries sync with Supabase under your account.

Selected situations can reveal health-related information. Selecting a situation is optional. Signed-out selections are stored locally and kept separate from account selections. When signed in, selections and saved entries sync using that account’s local cache and server records. The reference calculations run in the app, but that does not mean all related preferences stay only on your device.

4. Public community discussions

Threads and replies, author display names, timestamps, and associated identifiers are publicly accessible, including to people who are not signed in. Avoid posting private health details, contact information, or information that identifies someone else.

Helpful-vote totals are public. The app shows your own selection to you when signed in. Deleting your thread removes its title, body, and author attribution and leaves a placeholder so other people’s replies remain. Deleting your reply does not delete other people’s responses. A public post may already have been copied by other readers.

5. Feedback and comment reports

Feedback you submit is sent through Formspree to the configured feedback inbox. It can include your selected view, rating, reaction, comments, and any name or email you choose to provide.

A comment report includes the reason you enter and identifying details about the reported discussion and comment, including its text, author information, identifiers, and timestamps. Reports also use Formspree. Do not submit sensitive medical records or urgent requests through feedback or reports.

6. Hosting and other service providers

Vercel hosts the website, Supabase provides accounts and database storage, and Formspree handles feedback and reports. These services may process technical request information such as IP addresses, browser details, and service logs when providing or securing their services. Google processes information when you choose Google sign-in. Their policies describe their own practices:

  • Vercel privacy notice
  • Supabase privacy policy
  • Formspree privacy policy
  • Google privacy policy

Links to research, products, and other resources take you to independent websites with their own privacy practices.

7. Browser storage and shared devices

The website uses browser storage for preferences, saved entries, and authentication. Signing out ends the app session but does not clear all local preferences. Clearing your browser’s site data removes locally stored information and signs you out; it does not delete information already stored in your account or public posts. On a shared device, sign out and clear site data when appropriate.

8. Retention, choices, and requests

Account records and synced preferences are stored to provide account features. You can change selected situations, remove saved entries, and delete your own community content using the controls described above. Feedback, reports, service logs, and backups may remain in the services that process them.

For access, correction, account deletion, or other privacy requests, contact Dyad Health Collective through dyadhealthcollective.com. There is no self-service account-deletion button in the current website. We may need to verify that a request concerns your account. Rights and any applicable exceptions depend on the laws that apply to your request.

9. Children and health information

GalactoGuide is intended for adults, including parents, caregivers, and healthcare providers. Do not create an account for a child or share a child’s identifying medical information in public discussions. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us.

GalactoGuide is an educational reference, not a patient portal or a channel for clinical care. Using its account or community features does not establish a patient-provider relationship.

10. Security and updates

The website uses HTTPS connections, authentication, and database access controls. No website or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. We will update this page when the website’s data practices change; the date above identifies this version.
